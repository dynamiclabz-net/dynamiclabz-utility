# Dynamic Labz Utility
### Sales. Projects. People. All in One Place.

**AgencyFlow** is an internal business management platform developed for managing the complete operational workflow of an IT agency — from acquiring prospects and converting them into clients to managing projects, tracking team activities, and monitoring business performance.

One centralized workspace. Complete visibility. Better execution.

---

## 🏢 About the Platform

AgencyFlow brings three major operational areas together:

- **Sales Management** – Manage prospects, calls, follow-ups, proposals, and client conversions.
- **Project Management** – Manage clients, projects, tasks, deadlines, documents, and invoices.
- **Team Management** – Track attendance, daily work logs, employee targets, and performance.

## 👥 User Roles

The platform operates with three access levels.

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| **Admin** | Complete system access, team management, sales monitoring, project oversight, attendance, and reports. |
| **Sales** | Manage assigned prospects, call logs, follow-ups, leads, proposals, and conversions. |
| **Developer** | Access assigned projects, manage tasks, update progress, and submit daily work logs. |

### Access Control

- Prospects are visible only to their creator and Admin.
- Sales users can manage their own sales pipeline.
- Developers can access their assigned projects and tasks.
- Admin has complete visibility across the platform.
- All access permissions are enforced at the application level.

## 📊 Core Modules

### 01. Sales CRM

Manage the complete sales journey:

`Prospect → Lead → Client`

**Prospect Management**
- Company name and location
- Website and business information
- Contact person and phone number
- Additional notes and details

**Call Management**
- Maintain individual call logs.
- Record discussion summaries.
- Track call outcomes.
- Schedule follow-ups.
- Maintain complete interaction history.

**Call Outcomes**
- Interested
- Not Interested
- Call Later
- Currently Unavailable
- No Response
- Meeting Scheduled

**Lead Management**
- Convert prospects into leads.
- Record meeting discussions.
- Track proposal submissions.
- Upload proposals and supporting documents.
- Maintain deal status and next actions.

**Client Conversion**
- Convert successful leads into clients.
- Create associated project records.
- Maintain client and project information.

### 02. Follow-up Dashboard

A centralized dashboard for daily sales activities.

- Today's follow-ups
- Upcoming follow-ups
- Overdue follow-ups
- Recent calls and interactions
- Active prospects and leads
- Sales pipeline overview

### 03. Client & Project Management

Every client gets a dedicated workspace.

- Client information and contact details
- Project information and status
- Assigned team members
- Project deadlines
- Task management
- Task assignments and progress
- Project-related documents
- Invoice and payment records

### 04. Document Management

Keep all business documents organized.

**Sales Documents**
- Proposals
- Requirements
- Meeting documents
- Other supporting files

**Client Documents**
- Invoices
- Agreements
- Project documents
- Other client-related records

### 05. Targets & Performance

Monitor individual and overall sales performance.

- Assign employee targets.
- Track prospect acquisition.
- Monitor calls and meetings.
- Track proposals and conversions.
- Review individual performance.
- View consolidated sales reports.

### 06. Attendance & Timesheets

Simple daily attendance and work tracking.

**Employee Actions**
- Check In
- Check Out
- Submit daily work summary
- Record completed activities

**Admin Dashboard**
- Daily attendance overview
- Employee check-in and check-out times
- Daily work summaries
- Monthly attendance reports
- Team activity monitoring

## 🔄 Platform Workflow

### Sales Journey

1. Add a new prospect.
2. Record calls and discussions.
3. Schedule follow-ups.
4. Track follow-up activities from the dashboard.
5. Convert qualified prospects into leads.
6. Record meetings and upload proposals.
7. Convert successful leads into clients.
8. Create projects and manage client documentation.

### Project Delivery

1. Create a client project.
2. Assign developers and team members.
3. Create tasks with deadlines.
4. Track task progress.
5. Monitor project delivery.
6. Maintain project documents and invoices.

### Team Operations

1. Employee checks in.
2. Works on assigned tasks.
3. Submits a daily work summary.
4. Checks out.
5. Admin reviews daily and monthly activity.
